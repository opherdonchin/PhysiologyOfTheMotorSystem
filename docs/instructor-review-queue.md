# Instructor review queue

A compact index of unresolved instructor decisions, not another authority. Each
question lives in its owning document; options, recommendations and evidence are
added there as the question is investigated, and may not yet exist. Accepted
answers are recorded in the owning document; this index then records resolution
by link. Silence is not approval, and agent assessment is not acceptance. Only
the instructor makes consequential decisions; agents may record a decision and
mark the item resolved after explicit approval is documented in the owning
artifact.

Operating convention (reused from PML): questions are collected without
interrupting the instructor, independent work continues, and one prioritized
question is presented when the instructor asks for review.
